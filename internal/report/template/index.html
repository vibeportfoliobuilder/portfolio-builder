Assumptions

  • Yields and growth come from derived.yaml for stocks (5-year dividend CAGR, else 3-year) and from the broker's fund data (broker.yaml) for funds, unless the mix file states them.
  • Withholding is the rate the broker keeps for the payer's country, from the country and broker packs. No local tax is modelled inside the tax-advantaged account.
  • Reinvestment: each currency's dividends buy the fund lines that trade in it, in proportion to their weights (USD into USD lines, EUR into EUR lines); a currency your strategy lists under stock_reinvest_currencies goes back into the stocks bought in it. Income with no line in its currency is converted at the broker pack's FX cost into the distributing funds. Stocks are otherwise bought only with new money. An accumulating fund counts as 0% income and takes only its own currency.
  • Yield on cost is next year's net dividend over the money paid in; "start money" is the same for the start amount alone.
  • Switch to income: at the end of year 10, 15 or 20 (the income starts the next January). Sold: growth and all-cap funds, and stocks in the Growth group, the growth bucket or yielding under 2%; a line's switch: keep|sell in the mix overrides. Bonds take up to the cap of the portfolio value, pay their yield with no tax and no growth, and are bought in their own currency (the broker's FX cost on foreign proceeds); the rest buys the mix's dividend stocks and income-bucket funds at their weights, net yields and growth (FX cost where the currency changes). Bond money is assumed to be free of tax when it leaves the account. The glide sells the same share of the growth holdings at each of its year ends and at the switch (60% in all), buying payers only; contributions keep buying every line. Income growth over the next 10 years is the dividends' own growth with bonds flat, compared with the inflation target.
  • Fees are pb's broker-pack price list plus any transaction tax, for the unedited mixes only; an edited mix is fee-checked by pb at the gate.
  • Real values divide by the inflation you set, compounded yearly. The default is the country pack's long-run inflation, kept on purpose as the target dividend growth must beat after year 10; it is not current inflation (see data/macro.yaml).
